# Example projects

Generated by `npm run examples` from synthetic, formant-synthesised vocal-like recordings (no real voices are included).
Open a `.vcs` folder with **Project ▸ Open…** in the desktop app, or load the same examples from **Project ▸ Example projects** (they are rebuilt in memory).
Each folder also contains `preview_*.wav`, an offline render of its arrangement (32 kHz / 16-bit to keep the repository small).

- **A · Ethereal Vocal Hook** – `Ethereal Vocal Hook.vcs` – Sung phrase → vowels & syllables → floating melodic hook with gaps → six candidates → favourite applied. (38 events, 4 layers, peak -8.2 dBFS)
- **B · Playful Electronic Chops** – `Playful Electronic Chops.vcs` – Sharp syllables → syncopated riff → stutters → final bar regenerated with the opening motif preserved. (120 events, 4 layers, peak -8.0 dBFS)
- **C · Ambient Ghost Vocals** – `Ambient Ghost Vocals.vcs` – Hums, breaths & whispers → sparse overlapping layers, long rests, slow change → export layers as stems. (20 events, 4 layers, peak -15.4 dBFS)
- **D · Vocal Phrase Reconstruction** – `Vocal Phrase Reconstruction.vcs` – Spoken phrase → words → reordered / repeated / removed / fragmented, one anchor word kept recognisable. (25 events, 2 layers, peak -9.4 dBFS)
- **E · Two-Voice Conversation** – `Two-Voice Conversation.vcs` – Two recordings → call-and-response; switch relation to alternating, interrupt, imitate, overlap… (48 events, 2 layers, peak -7.3 dBFS)
- **F · MIDI-Guided Vocal Pattern** – `MIDI-Guided Vocal Pattern.vcs` – Vocal sample + MIDI riff → slices triggered on MIDI timing, velocity accents, pitch by playback rate. (46 events, 1 layers, peak -11.9 dBFS)

## media/

- `sung-phrase.wav` – A floating sung line: "la-la oh yeah, ah ooh" with sustained vowels.
- `spoken-phrase.wav` – Spoken word-like syllables with consonant attacks: "take me / to the / light to-night / now".
- `hum-whisper.wav` – Sustained hums, breaths and whispered "haa / shh" for ghost-vocal textures.
- `voice-b.wav` – A brighter second voice with sharp syllables: "hey / yeah / oh-oh / go / ti-ka".
- `chop-attacks.wav` – Punchy short syllables for rhythmic chops: "ti ka ta pa chi da".
- `garage-riff.mid` – an 8-bar 2-step style MIDI riff for the MIDI-guided workflow.
